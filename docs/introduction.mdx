`jev-curate` streams, filters, and scores millions of Parquet and JSONL rows without rewriting a single character.

## Key Capabilities

- **Single-round-trip speculative fan-out** per row.
- **Calibrated judgments:** `Noul`, `Score`, and `Choice` outputs.
- **Bounded streaming:** Resumable pipelines with `--resume`.
- **Native Parquet & JSONL:** Opt-in `--format parquet` output backed by Apache Arrow.
